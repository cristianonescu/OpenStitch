## What the sew-outs found and what changed

The owner sewed the ready-to-sew kit on a Brother NV880E (New Brothread thread, pink cotton).

- **PES cut off on the machine (#286, #287).** The PEC header's frame was the design's size while
  the stitches kept their page position, so the machine cut every design by its page margin.
  #286 then gave such designs PE-DESIGN's 290 × 190 mm frame, which the NV880E refused as too
  large for its largest hoop. #287 made the frame the design's own size with the stitches from its
  top-left corner (PE-DESIGN's design-sized files). The compat runner puts read-back exports back
  at their page position before measuring.
- **Stitching in place (#286):** stippling sewed 0.2 mm steps (now the Run Pitch); the flexible
  spiral crowded its centre (now at least 0.5 mm apart).
- **`tools/sew_check.py`:** reads a PES as the machine does and fails a frame larger than the
  hoop, stitches outside the frame, more than 20 drops in a 0.5 mm spot, more than 80 mm of thread
  per mm², or more than 6 stitches under 0.3 mm in a row (limits from PE-DESIGN's own files).
- **Other machine formats:** DST, EXP, JEF, PCS, HUS, VIP, SEW and XXX headers compared with
  PE-DESIGN's exports of the same designs: the size and position fields match. JEF's third hoop
  slot holds sizes where PE-DESIGN writes −1 (left as a known difference).
- **Calibration:** the first T1 card result (swatch colours, coverage ramp) is in the kit README's
  *Calibration results*, with the record in `examples/sew/calibration/results/`. A GitHub issue
  form, *Sew-out / calibration report*, and the kit's *Sending results* say what testers send.

## Lessons (also in the coordinator's memory)

- Check every PES with `tools/sew_check.py` before it reaches a machine.
- Model everything the machine does with a field, not only the bug at hand: a frame must both
  hold the stitches and fit the hoop.
- When PE-DESIGN's files show two conventions, choose the one that is safe on the machine.
- One build or release-binary job at a time on Windows; check the build succeeded before using it.
