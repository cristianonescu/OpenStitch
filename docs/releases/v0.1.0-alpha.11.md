# OpenStitch 0.1.0 alpha 11

The eleventh alpha of OpenStitch, an independent, cross-platform embroidery design application for
macOS, Windows and Linux, built to work with Brother machines and with designs made in
PE-DESIGN 11. OpenStitch is not affiliated with Brother Industries.

Alpha 10 adds a **ready-to-sew kit**: 35 embroidery files made by OpenStitch, ready to copy to a
machine, packaged with the editor and opened from **Help ▸ Ready-to-Sew Designs**. The stitch
output is unchanged from alpha 9. **It is still an alpha for testing, not for production work.**
Read "Before you sew" below.

Check for Updates. Alpha 3 and earlier must be replaced by hand with a package above.

## The builds are not code-signed

- **macOS:** the first time, right-click `OpenStitch.app` and choose **Open**, then **Open**
  again. Alternatively, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/OpenStitch.app`.
- **Windows:** in the SmartScreen prompt, choose **More info → Run anyway**.

## What changed since alpha 10

### Fixed: PES files are cut off on the machine

Every PES and PEC file OpenStitch wrote (File ▸ Export, Send to USB, Design Database convert,
Name Drop, the PE-DESIGN 11 format and the ready-to-sew kit) told the machine the design was only
as large as itself, while its stitches kept their position on the Design Page. A Brother machine
sews only inside that frame, so it **cut every design off by its page margin**: on a Brother
NV880E, lettering lost its last letters and the bottom of a circle text, and satin columns were
pressed into a line at the edge.

The frame is now the design's own size, with the stitches starting at its top-left corner, as in
PE-DESIGN's design-sized files. The machine accepts the design in the smallest hoop it fits and
sews it whole. **Export your designs again** from alpha 11 before sewing them; files exported
from alpha 10 or earlier keep the problem.

The other machine formats (DST, EXP, JEF, PCS, HUS, VIP, SEW, XXX) did not have it: their size and
position fields were checked against PE-DESIGN's own exports of the same designs and match.

### Fixed: stitching in place

- **Stippling** (also in the Background Fill wizard) sewed its meander in 0.2 mm steps, hundreds of
  stitches hammering almost the same spot. It now sews at the region's Run Pitch.
- **Flexible spiral** crowded its stitches under 0.3 mm towards the centre; they are now at least
  0.5 mm apart.

### Ready-to-sew kit

- Regenerated with these fixes. Every calibration design and machine check fits the small
  100 × 100 mm hoop.
- **Calibration results:** the first T1 card, sewn on a Brother NV880E with New Brothread thread,
  scanned and measured (swatch colours and the coverage ramp), in the kit's README. Results will
  change as OpenStitch improves.
- **Help test on your machine:** a new
  [Sew-out / calibration report](https://github.com/cristianonescu/OpenStitch/issues/new?template=sew-out-report.yml)
  form on GitHub, and the kit's *Sending results* says what to include (machine, hoop, version,
  thread, needle, fabric, scanner settings, the original scans). So far only the NV880E has sewn
  the kit; reports from other machines, especially for the non-PES formats, are very welcome.

### Tools

- `tools/sew_check.py` checks embroidery files the way a machine reads them: a frame larger than
  the hoop, stitches outside the frame, needle drops piling up in one spot, and stitching in place.

## Before you sew

- **Do a test sew-out on scrap fabric** before using a design on anything that matters.
- **Re-export designs you exported with alpha 10 or earlier** before sewing them (see above).
- **Known open items:** the large storybook pictures in the kit stack up to 29 needle drops where
  satin columns meet, against at most 19 in PE-DESIGN's own files (left out of the recommended
  sew-outs until fixed); small satin lettering ends a stroke in very short stitches; the JEF
  header's third hoop slot holds sizes where PE-DESIGN writes "none". Other known differences from
  PE-DESIGN 11 are in the README's "Known issues" and
  [docs/COMPATIBILITY_AUDIT.md](../COMPATIBILITY_AUDIT.md).
- **Keep your original PE-DESIGN files.** Save OpenStitch's output under a new name.
- **macOS:** double-clicking a file in Finder does not open it in OpenStitch yet.

## Feedback

differently from PE-DESIGN 11. For sew-outs, use the
[Sew-out / calibration report](https://github.com/cristianonescu/OpenStitch/issues/new?template=sew-out-report.yml)
