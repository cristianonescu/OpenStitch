## Changed output: the same design can sew differently

These defaults changed. Each item says how to get the old behaviour back where there is a way.

- **A fill with under sewing starts 1 mm inside its outline (#1088):**
  - **What changed:** a Fill region with under sewing used to start on its outline, at its
    entry point, and step 1 mm inward to the under sewing. It now starts directly on the under
    sewing's edge run 1 mm inside the outline, at the point nearest the entry, as PE-DESIGN
    11.50 does in all its references. On the machine, the first needle point of such a region
    is 1 mm inside the shape instead of on its edge, and the region has one or two needle
    points fewer.
  - **Also changed: the way into the under sewing's rows.** After its first round 1 mm
    inside the outline, the under sewing used to cut straight across the shape to its first
    row. It now goes on round the edge, the way it was going, to the nearest outermost row.
    It then sews the rows in two halves, from each side in towards where it started, and ends
    there, as PE-DESIGN does. On the machine, no long under sewing stitch crosses the shape
    diagonally any more. The same rows are sewn, in a different order.
  - **Not affected:** fills without under sewing, other region types, and under sewing with a
    centre run (piping). The start only moves on regions with an entry point; regions without
    one already started on the edge run. On a 5 mm square, whose under sewing is a 3 mm loop,
    OpenStitch now walks on the same way while PE-DESIGN turns back; that case is not followed
    yet (#1088).
  - **Old behaviour:** none.
