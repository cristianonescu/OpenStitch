## What changed since alpha 14

### Editor

- An inspector field shows a stored value outside its range as it is (a zigzag net from an older
  file can hold a 0.6 mm Spacing where the field runs from 2.0): selecting the object no longer
  changes the value, and a value you type or drag is still kept to the range (#1109).
- Inspector fields now hold every value their setting can hold, with PE-DESIGN 11.50's own
  ranges where it has the setting (#1124). In alpha 14, selecting an object rewrote a value
  outside the field's range, so a design saved after that may hold the rewritten value:
  - **Candlewick line Density** is now PE-DESIGN's drop-down: Dense, Medium (the default) and
    Light, knots of 32, 24 and 20 points. A density set by command to another value is shown
    as it is. A candlewick line you selected in alpha 14 was changed from Medium knots (24
    points each) to Light knots (20 points), and sews that way if you saved it: select it and
    choose Medium in Density. Size runs 3.0–10.0 mm in the inspector, PE-DESIGN's range, and
    0.5–10 mm by `object.setLine` as in alpha 14; a smaller knot set by command is shown as it
    is and kept until you edit it. Spacing runs 0.0–100.0 mm, PE-DESIGN's range, in both.
  - **Decorative Fill Pattern width and height** run to 500 mm, PE-DESIGN's maximum, in the
    inspector and in `object.setRegion` (they stopped at 50 and 100). PE-DESIGN's range starts at
    10 mm; OpenStitch's own tiles may be smaller, from 0.5 mm. A decorative fill opened
    from a PE-DESIGN file and selected in alpha 14 may have been saved with a 50 mm tile instead
    of PE-DESIGN's 100: type 100 in Pattern width and height.
  - **Maze Spacing** starts at 0.5 mm, as the command. A maze set below 1.0 mm by command and
    selected in alpha 14 was saved at 1.0: type the spacing again.
  - **Knockdown:** the second layer of a two-layer knockdown at an angle of 270° or more is
    stored as the same direction within ±360° (450 as 90, a full turn as 0), so the Direction
    field can show it.
  - **E stitch and V stitch Run times** run 1–5, the guide's range; the field offered 6, which
    the command refused.

### Files

- **PES version 11 export: a closed line starts in PE-DESIGN where it starts in OpenStitch
  (#1139).** PE-DESIGN 11.50 sews a closed line from the outline's first vertex. OpenStitch wrote
  a rectangle's corners from the top-left, so PE-DESIGN, regenerating the stitches of an exported
  motif square, started its line at that corner, not at the bottom centre. A closed outline is now
  written from its line's start point (without a line, its region's): a rectangle as PE-DESIGN's
  Rectangle tool stores it, five vertices from the bottom centre. This also moves the first
  vertex of rounded rectangles, of the star, heart, triangle, diamond and teardrop presets, and
  of any shape whose start point was moved. The stitches OpenStitch writes do not change, and
  shapes opened from PE-DESIGN files keep their vertices.

## Changed output: the same design can sew differently

These defaults changed. Each item says how to get the old behaviour back where there is a way.

- **Check Design judges small text by its own font (#1036):**
  - **What changed:** `textTooSmall` warned under 4 mm for every font. It now uses the text's own
    font's smallest good size: Lato Bold 6 mm, Pacifico 7 mm, OpenStitch Line (Small Text) 3 mm,
    OpenStitch Pixel 5 mm (each measured against `sew_check`'s limits: 1 mm smaller sews hairline
    satins), or the range an embroidery font's maker states. Its fix switches to the Small Text
    font where the text is big enough for it, else sets the font's smallest size.
  - **The kit:** `lettering/fonts-and-text.pes`'s ring text "ROUND AND ROUND" was 5 mm Lato Bold,
    under its 6 mm, and is now 6 mm: 3,980 → 4,176 stitches, 71 × 89.7 → 71 × 90.7 mm, 38 trims
    as before. None of PE-DESIGN's 497 PE-DESIGN 11 reference files trips the new limits.
  - **Old behaviour:** none; a text kept small on purpose gets a warning, never a refusal.

- **Every section start ties in, also after a lone needle point or a 0.1 mm first stitch (#1076):**
  - **What changed:** two kinds of run started without a tie-in that holds:
    - A run whose first piece is a single needle point, with the run's next piece close enough to be
      stitched to (an Auto Punch satin piece in the OpenStitch-mode storybook). The section opened
      with an untied 2.7 mm stitch. The point now starts the next piece, which ties in on it.
    - A run whose first stitch is between 0.1 and 0.15 mm (an Auto Punch fill in the
      PE-compatible storybook). It tied in by stepping that far and back, and a machine file's
      0.1 mm grid rounded the step onto its start. The tie-in now steps along the first stitch
      longer than 0.15 mm, which always lands on another grid point.
  - **What a sewer notices:** the kit's two Auto Punch storybooks had one thread end without a
    tie each and have none, as built and as exported PES. In the kit:
    - `image-tools/L-autopunch-storybook-openstitch.pes`: 95,056 → 95,061 stitches;
    - `image-tools/L-autopunch-storybook-pe-compatible.pes`: 113,916 → 113,921;
    - `fills/meander-tiles-2.pes`: 2,181 → 2,182;
    - `shapes/shape-tools.pes`: 4,484 → 4,485;
    - `lettering/monograms.pes`: needle points moved by a tie-in step, count unchanged (7,606).
    No trim is added or removed.
  - **Not affected:** a lone point farther from the next piece than a sewn connection (4 mm),
    which stays a move's landing as before, and lone points later in a run (a pulled fill row's
    end).
  - **Old behaviour:** none.

- **Mitre and cap corners on satin lines and auto-direction satins (#1040, #1048):**
  - **What changed:** OpenStitch's corner styles (beyond PE-DESIGN, #520) read a corner from the
    rungs next to it. A satin line has a rung every half millimetre, so a sharp corner read
    blunt (a 36° star tip as 53°) and a cap never fitted between rungs that close: Cap sewed
    as Mitre, and Cap below and Cap max length changed nothing. On an auto-direction satin the
    skeleton's long slanted rungs lay across the cap's cut, with the same result. Corners are
    now read from the rungs a leg's length away, and a cap reaches to the first rungs wholly
    behind and ahead of its cut. On a five-point-star satin line: Mitre 592 → 621 stitches,
    Cap 592 → 616 (now its own); on a narrow V auto-direction satin, Cap 860 → 830.
  - **Not affected:** the default corner style (Turn, PE-DESIGN's), and corners of Manual
    Punch columns and outlines whose rungs are already a leg apart. No kit design uses these
    styles.
  - **Old behaviour:** none; Turn sews as before.

- **Satin under sewing: no separate "Edge and fill" (#1041):** on a satin, PE-DESIGN's first
  under sewing type is the edge run and zigzag rows; edge and fill is that same button on a fill.
  The satin's Underlay style no longer offers Edge and fill, and a satin that held it (asked for,
  or kept from a fill switched to a satin) shows and keeps Edge and zigzag. No stitches change.
- **Zigzag net: no rung crosses the next on a turned shape (#1058):**
  - **What changed:** where a zigzag net's line ends on the outline and the outline cuts the
    last rung of the band it goes into, the needle went to the line's end and back across that
    rung, so two stitches crossed (on a circle turned by some angles). It now goes straight on,
    leaving that needle point out. No rung crosses the next for the square, diamond, triangle and
    honeycomb patterns on a circle turned every 5° from 0° to 85°. The ready-to-sew kit's
    `fills/region-sew-types-2.pes` (a honeycomb zigzag net) goes from 4,665 to 4,658 stitches.
  - **Old behaviour:** none.
- **A motif line opened from PE-DESIGN sews at its own Run pitch (#1068):**
  - **What changed:** a motif line read from a PE-DESIGN file took the running stitch pitch
    PE-DESIGN keeps from its running line tool (2.0 mm) instead of the motif line's own Run pitch
    (5.0 mm by default). At 2.0 mm every motif stitch longer than that was split in two: PE-DESIGN's
    40 mm square with a 5 × 8 mm motif sewed 118 stitches in OpenStitch against PE-DESIGN's 75.
    It now sews 77; at Run pitch 3.0, 118 (PE-DESIGN 117), and at 7.0, 77 (PE-DESIGN 75).
    Start/End Type Run is read too.
  - **Also:** a motif line exported to PES version 11 writes its Run pitch and Start/End Type to
    the fields PE-DESIGN's own files keep them in. PE-DESIGN 11.50 opens such a file with the Run
    pitch and Start/End Type OpenStitch wrote and sews the line itself: at Run pitch 3.0, 116
    needle points (OpenStitch 118), and at 5.0 with Run, 74 (OpenStitch 77).
  - **Not affected:** motif lines drawn in OpenStitch; the kit (its designs are built in
    OpenStitch).
  - **Old behaviour:** none.
- **A plain net starts from the shape (#1071):**
  - **What changed:** a Net Fill region's lines start half a spacing in from the top-left corner
    of the shape's bounds, as PE-DESIGN 11.50's do, not from the page origin; and the needle
    steps the run pitch (2 mm) along each whole line, through its crossings, instead of from
    crossing to crossing (1.5 mm stitches at 3 mm spacing). On a 30 × 20 mm rectangle at
    Spacing 3.0 the needle points inside the shape are now PE-DESIGN's at 0°, 45° and 90°:
    280, 315 and 278 stitches against PE-DESIGN's 281, 336 and 281 (before: 344, 374, 316).
    The ready-to-sew kit's `fills/region-sew-types-2.pes` (a square net) goes from 4,675 to
    4,665 stitches, and `wizards/cutwork.pes` (the Cutwork wizard's net) from 3,541 to 3,303.
  - **Not affected:** a net with a reference point, which still starts there.
  - **Old behaviour:** none; set a reference point to place the net.

- **Text Transform: envelope edges without ripples between letters (#1079):**
  - **What changed:** OpenStitch's Text Transform envelopes are fitted from PE-DESIGN 11.50's
    HHHHHHH captures. Where no letter stem lay near a point of an edge (between two H's), the
    fit's smooth curve rippled: preset 18's straight bottom edge dipped by up to 4 % of the
    letter height under the second letter. Such a point now lies on the straight line between
    its neighbours. "OpenStitch" in Lato Bold 10 mm under preset 18 at level 100: p's
    descender reached 50.9 mm, PE-DESIGN 51.5; now 51.4. Presets 15 to 26 change by under
    0.06 of the letter height at these points.
  - **Not affected:** presets 1 to 14, arched text and free envelopes that do not start from a
    preset.
  - **Old behaviour:** none.
- **Gradation: no "Steps" control (#1039):** PE-DESIGN's Gradation Pattern Setting always has nine
  points (checked in PE-DESIGN 11.50, round 15), and OpenStitch's rows follow the fall from Max to
  Min whatever the count, so the Steps control, which changed nothing, is gone. A script or design
  that still passes `gradationSteps` is accepted and gets a hint that it is ignored; on its own it
  no longer turns a gradation pattern on. No stitches change.
- **Radial fill as PE-DESIGN sews it, denser at higher densities (#1050):**
  - **What changed:** Radial Stitch now sews its lines in pairs, in to a point and back out to
    the outline, 1/density apart at the outline at every density, with about 16 pairs a turn
    reaching the centre and the others stopping at a long or a short radius in turn, as
    PE-DESIGN 11.50 does. Before, the lines never came closer than 0.4 mm, so any density above
    about 2.5 lines/mm sewed the same. On a 40 mm circle: 2,149 stitches at every density before;
    2,239, 3,346, 5,526 and 7,731 at 2, 3, 5 and 7 lines/mm now (PE-DESIGN 2,311, 3,423, 5,717
    and 7,988).
  - **Density range:** 0.2 to 7.0 lines/mm, PE-DESIGN's own (it was 0.5 to 10).
  - **Default density:** 2.0 lines/mm, PE-DESIGN's own (it was 1.5).
  - **In the kit:** `fills/region-sew-types-1.pes` (its radial shape) 7,130 → 7,173 stitches (the new default density),
    no trim added or removed.
  - **Not affected:** other region types.
  - **Old behaviour:** the old default density: set `density` to 1.5. The old line layout:
    none.
- **A fill's under sewing follows PE-DESIGN's path (#1088):**
  - **What changed:** a Fill region with under sewing now sews its under sewing along the path
    PE-DESIGN 11.50 sews. A lone 5 mm square now sews PE-DESIGN's 85 needle points; it was 82.
    - It starts on the run 1 mm inside the outline, not on the outline: at the point nearest the
      entry, or without an entry nearest the shape's first point.
    - It goes once round that inner run, then straight to the far side's outermost row.
    - It sews the rows from there in towards where it started, in two halves when it started in
      the middle. The walks between rows are in stitches of at most 2.5 mm.
    - It ends where it started and steps out to the entry on the outline before the top layer.
    - Before, it started on the outline, stepped in, and crossed the shape diagonally to its
      first row.
  - **What a sewer notices:** the first needle point of such a region is 1 mm inside the shape;
    the under sewing no longer crosses the shape in long diagonal stitches; a fill's needle
    count moves by a few, mostly up.
  - **Not affected:** fills without under sewing, other region types, under sewing with a
    centre run (piping), and the letters of a text. No PE-DESIGN capture shows a letter's under
    sewing, so letters keep the earlier path until one does, as their joins (#1082). Fills with
    holes, and shapes where a row walk would leave the region (a star's points), keep it too.
  - **Known gaps:** with the under sewing nearer PE-DESIGN's, the top fill's own difference
    shows in the totals: 15 mm squares sew 12 to 15 needle points more than PE-DESIGN, a U shape
    45 to 64 fewer (#1131).
- **Zigzag net: Spacing is the gap between bands (#1109):**
  - **What changed:** a Zigzag Net Fill's Spacing is now the gap between bands, as in
    PE-DESIGN 11.50, not the distance between the net lines: the lines are the zigzag width
    plus the Spacing apart, and the band is no longer narrowed to fit the Spacing. The default
    Spacing is 5.0 mm (with the 3.0 mm band, lines 8.0 mm apart as before). The inspector's
    ranges are PE-DESIGN's: zigzag width 1.0–10.0 mm, Spacing 2.0–10.0 mm. PES files from
    PE-DESIGN 11 import and export with the same meaning. The ready-to-sew kit is unchanged.
  - **Not affected:** designs saved before this version (.osd version 5 or older) load with
    Spacing set to the old line distance minus the band, and sew as before.
  - **Old behaviour:** to place lines a given distance apart, set Spacing to that distance
    minus the zigzag width (`object.setRegion`'s `spacing` likewise).
- **A plain net's default Spacing is 3.0 mm (#1110):**
  - **What changed:** a Net Fill region with no Spacing of its own now sews its lines 3.0 mm
    apart, PE-DESIGN 11.50's default (its Spacing field's reset button), not 4.0 mm. The
    ready-to-sew kit's `fills/region-sew-types-2.pes` (a square net) goes from 4,658 to 4,727
    stitches.
  - **Not affected:** designs saved before this version (.osd version 4 or older): a net that
    left Spacing unset loads with 4.0 mm and sews as before. A net with its own Spacing keeps it.
  - **Old behaviour:** set Spacing to 4.0.

- **A motif line's default Run pitch is 5.0 mm (#1130):**
  - **What changed:** a new motif line sews its runs at 5.0 mm, PE-DESIGN 11.50's default (its
    Run pitch field's reset button), not 2.5 mm. The Run pitch sews the runs between motifs and
    splits motif segments longer than it, so a line with a gap, or a motif with long segments,
    sews fewer stitches. Changing a line's type to motif gives it 5.0 unless `pitch` comes in
    the same call.
  - **Example scripts:** line-type-motif 402 → 381 stitches, line-type-motif-groups 332 → 326,
    line-type-motif-in-turn 315 → 313, line-type-motif-mirror-alternate 315 → 313, line-types
    2,573 → 2,552, motif-catalogue 2,794 → 2,400, ornaments 3,261 → 3,260, user-patterns
    2,246 → 2,208.
  - **Not affected:** designs saved before this version keep the Run pitch they were saved with
    (every .osd stores it). Motif lines opened from a PES keep the Run pitch in the file. A
    motif line with no gap whose motif segments are all under 2.5 mm sews the same, as the
    ready-to-sew kit's line-types wave does.
  - **Old behaviour:** set Run pitch to 2.5.

- **A motif line cuts a sharp corner with one motif (#1123):**
  - **What changed:** a motif that would span a sharp corner (30° or more) of the line is now
    sewn straight across it, as PE-DESIGN 11.50 does: its ends lie one motif width apart, on
    either side of the corner, with no needle point on the corner. A run between motifs that
    crosses a corner follows the outline, corner point included, its ends one gap apart. The
    number of motifs counts the shortcut at each corner, and one scale for every motif fills
    the line.
  - **Against PE-DESIGN:** the 40 mm square of 608-motif-5x8 sews 75 needle points, as
    PE-DESIGN's, where it sewed 77; at Run pitch 3, 117 as PE-DESIGN's (118), and the longest
    stitch is 2.10 mm, as PE-DESIGN's (2.36). The top-left corner is (0, 3.1) → (3.2, 3.1) →
    (3.9, 0) mm against PE-DESIGN's (0, 3.3) → (3.2, 3.1) → (3.8, 0).
  - **Not affected:** lines without a sharp corner (straight lines, circles, curves) sew as
    before; motif lines with groups, free ends or motifs in turn (beyond PE-DESIGN) still run
    into a corner. No example script and no file of the ready-to-sew kit moves.
  - **Known gap:** PE-DESIGN gives the 0.3 mm left over on the square to its last motif and gap
    (5.2 and 2.1 mm); OpenStitch shares it over every motif.

- **Satin e: the bar turns into the bowl as PE-DESIGN sews it (#1080):**
  - **What changed:** on a satin letter whose stroke bends sharply with an outline corner on each
    side (e's bar turning up into its bowl), the two corners are joined by one crossing and the
    crossings near them are spaced along each side's length; a straight stretch ending in such a
    turn is spaced along its middle. Before, e's bar kept upright crossings and then pivoted 16 of
    them round the counter's corner. Lato Bold e at 20 mm: 281 stitches, PE-DESIGN 267; now 267.
  - **Only on strokes 1.2 mm wide or more** (their median width): small and script letters (4 mm
    Lato, 7 mm Pacifico) are as before.
  - **In the kit:** `lettering/fonts-and-text.pes` 4,176 → 4,145 stitches, no trim added or removed.
  - **Not affected:** fill lettering; satin letters without such a turn (no other stroke-satin
    reference moves, except K-8: 137 → 131, PE-DESIGN 128).
  - **Old behaviour:** none.
- **The under sewing's rows sew PE-DESIGN's needle points (#1132):**
  - **What changed:** the rows of a fill's under sewing now place their needle points as
    PE-DESIGN 11.50 does, row for row, at every density.
    - The 4 mm grid is anchored on the shape, like the top fill's, not on the page, and moves
      0.3 of a stitch per row from the edge run's side.
    - A row keeps short end stitches down to 0.3 mm, where it used to leave out points closer
      than 1 mm to its ends.
    - On a 40 mm square the under sewing's needle points are now PE-DESIGN's but one at the
      start (the point before the tie-in), at dense, default and light density:
      - dense: 388 → 400, PE-DESIGN 399;
      - default: 301 → 309, PE-DESIGN 308;
      - light: 224 → 229, PE-DESIGN 228.
    - The three sweeps now measure the under sewing's count on its own, within 1 of
      PE-DESIGN's.
    - The lone 5 mm square's diagonal row gains PE-DESIGN's point at (3.5, 3.5), so it sews 86
      needle points against PE-DESIGN's 85. The difference is the top fill's two extra points
      (#1131), less PE-DESIGN's zero-length repeat at a corner.
  - **What a sewer notices:** a fill's needle count goes up by a few. The under sewing lies under
    the top fill and looks the same.
  - **Not affected:** the top fill, and fills without under sewing.
- **A new programmable fill starts at density 4.5 (#1102):**
  - **What changed:**
    - A new Prog. Fill Stitch region now gets 4.5 lines/mm, PE-DESIGN 11.50's default (its
      Density field's reset button), not 2.5.
    - This holds for a new region and for a region switched to a programmable fill. A density
      given with the switch still wins.
    - The 45° capture's square sews 2,664 stitches, against PE-DESIGN's 2,686 (1,636 before).
  - **In the kit:**
    - `fills/region-sew-types-1.pes`: 7,216 → 7,520 stitches.
    - `fills/region-sew-types-2.pes`: 4,749 → 5,382 stitches.
    - No trim was added or removed, and sew_check is ok on both.
  - **Not affected:** designs saved before this version. Every region stores its density, so a
    programmable fill saved at 2.5 keeps 2.5.
  - **Old behaviour:** set Density to 2.5 (`object.setRegion`'s `density` likewise).
- **Check Design counts stitches in place as the machine file holds them (#1135):**
  - **What changed:** `inPlace` (more than 6 stitches in a row shorter than 0.3 mm) measured
    between the design's own needle points. It now measures between them rounded to the 0.1 mm
    grid a PES stores, turned with the hoop when the hoop is rotated 90°, so it reports the run
    the exported file sews. A 0.29 mm step that the file stores as 0.3 mm ends a run, and a
    0.3 mm diagonal that the file stores shorter extends one. `tools/sew_check.py` counts in
    whole 0.1 mm units too: it read an exact 0.3 mm step as 0.29999 mm and counted it.
  - **The kit:** `lettering/fonts-and-text.pes` (Check Design read 9 in a row, its file 6) is
    now Ready to sew, and `image-tools/L-autopunch-storybook-pe-compatible.pes` (10, its file
    6) is Sewable with small risks. No stitch changes.
  - **PE-DESIGN's references:** three of the 508 opened with `import.pes` read differently, each
    now the run in OpenStitch's own export: text-transform-19 at level 100, 3 → 7 (now
    reported); text-transform-25 at level 100, 8 → 5; zigzag mitre V, 7 → 3.
  - **`ui.state`'s `ready`** is now `design.check`'s: no errors. It also used to need no
    warnings, which `level`'s Ready to sew still does.
  - **Old behaviour:** none.
- **A closed straight-line shape starts and ends at its first corner (#1103):**
  - **What changed:**
    - A shape drawn with Closed Straight Line (`shape.polygon`) now starts and ends its region
      at the corner clicked first, as PE-DESIGN 11.50 does. This holds whichever corner comes
      first and in either drawing direction. Before, it started at the end of a fill row.
    - The diamond, star and triangle of `shape-start-points-outline` now start and end where
      PE-DESIGN's do, within 0.2 mm.
    - The U shapes (fill, satin and gradation) and the 120° satin band now end on their first
      corner, as PE-DESIGN's do. Each now has its own ends check.
    - Their stitch counts change by the walk back to that corner:
      - fill U: 1,235 → 1,240 (PE-DESIGN 1,254);
      - satin U: 484 → 492 (PE-DESIGN 466);
      - gradation U: 815 → 820 at 50/50, 828 → 857 at 80/20 (PE-DESIGN 811 and 819).
    - The counts that move away from PE-DESIGN do so because the top layer was already over
      (#1131, #1104). For the satin U, the left leg and the walk back sew more needle points
      than PE-DESIGN's.
  - **In the kit:** `fills/angle-lines.pes` goes from 4,683 to 4,723 stitches. No trim was
    added or removed, and sew_check is ok.
  - **Not affected:**
    - Designs saved before this version: their shapes keep the start and end they were
      saved with.
    - Rectangles, ellipses, gallery shapes and open lines.
  - **Old behaviour:** move the start and end with the Start/End Point tool
    (`object.setEntryExit`), or clear them (`null`) for a start at a row end.
- **Skeleton satin: the outline no longer folds over itself at a bend (#1136):**
  - **What changed:** a skeleton satin's outline joins the ends of its rows. On the inside of a
    tight bend those ends run backwards, so that side looped over itself, and Check Design
    reported the column as `selfCrossing`. Each side now cuts its loops at the point where it
    crosses itself, so the outline goes round the bend once.
  - **The kit:** `shapes/shape-tools.pes`' skeleton V (thread 7) sews 374 stitches instead of
    388, with the same trims. The design goes from Fix this first to Ready to sew. No other kit
    file changes; Auto Punch's outlines use the same code and sew as before.
  - **Old behaviour:** none. A file saved before alpha.15 still opens as a skeleton satin but
    keeps its folded outline, so its under sewing still follows the fold and Check Design
    still reports `selfCrossing`, until it is refreshed: select it with the Satin Skeleton tool
    and drag any width handle (even back to its width), or run `object.setSkeleton` with its
    points, widths and `reshape: true`. Its outline is then rebuilt without the fold.

- **Fill letters: no stitches in place where an envelope squeezes a stroke (#1151):**
  - **What changed:** a fill letter sews each stroke as rows across it, needle points on its two
    sides in turn. Where a Text Transform squeezes a stroke to 0.2-0.3 mm, every step across it
    was under 0.3 mm. "OpenStitch" in Lato Bold under preset 19 at level 100 sewed 7 such
    stitches in a row in its O, more than the machine's limit of 6, and Check Design reported
    it. Now each step goes on to the next needle point across the stroke at least 0.35 mm away,
    skipping at most four. Where every step is that long already, the letter sews as before.
  - **Against PE-DESIGN 11.50:** PE-DESIGN's file of that design has 555 stitches, the longest
    run under 0.3 mm 5. OpenStitch's went from 797 stitches and a run of 7 to 630 and 3.
    Other levels and presets, needle points (run):
    - preset 7 at level 100: 1,016 (22) → 958 (3), PE-DESIGN 900 (3);
    - preset 11 at level 100: 1,024 (23) → 971 (3), PE-DESIGN 906 (3);
    - preset 15 at level 100: 720 (7) → 542 (2), PE-DESIGN 521 (3);
    - preset 8 at level 100: 1,183 (9) → 1,123 (4), PE-DESIGN 929 (3);
    - preset 12 at level 100: 1,184 (8) → 1,130 (3), PE-DESIGN 940 (3).
  - **Every text spec:** of the 241 text designs exported, 159 change. 147 come closer to
    PE-DESIGN's stitch count and 12 move away by 1 to 12 stitches. No export's longest run
    under 0.3 mm grows.
  - **In the kit:** `lettering/monograms.pes` keeps its 7,708 stitches and its trims; its
